📊 Business KPI Dashboard – Customer Churn Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer subscription and churn data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides an overview of customer behaviour, retention, churn rate, and revenue at risk through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The objective is to help businesses understand customer churn patterns and identify areas where customer retention strategies may be improved.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer subscription patterns.
- Calculate customer churn and retention rates.
- Understand revenue at risk due to customer churn.
- Analyze customer tenure.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPI| Result
Total Customers| 15
Churn Rate| 46.67%
Retention Rate| 53.33%
Revenue at Risk| 409.93
Total Monthly Revenue| 1.27K
Average Tenure| Calculated in Power BI

📊 Dashboard Features

- Customer count by subscription type.
- Customer churn analysis by subscription type.
- Customer churn analysis by payment method, if available in the dataset.
- Customer tenure analysis.
- Interactive slicers for available customer attributes.
- KPI cards for quick business insights.

🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization

💡 Key Observations

- The sample dataset shows a churn rate of 46.67%.
- The retention rate is 53.33%.
- Revenue at risk is calculated using the monthly charges associated with churned customers.
- Subscription and tenure analysis help explore customer distribution and churn patterns.

⚠️ Dataset Limitation

The dataset contains only 15 customer records. Therefore, the results are illustrative and are not statistically representative of a larger customer population.
